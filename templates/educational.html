Educational Zoo Visits

Our zoo offers immersive and engaging educational visits designed to inspire students of all ages. Whether you're studying habitats, conservation, animal behaviour, or biodiversity, our sessions bring learning to life through real-world encounters and hands‑on experiences.

What Schools Can Expect

  • Interactive talks led by experienced education staff
  • Close‑up encounters with selected animals
  • Curriculum‑linked learning tailored to age groups
  • Access to all zoo exhibits for the full day
  • Designated lunch areas for school groups

Learning Themes

  • Habitats & Adaptations
  • Endangered Species & Conservation
  • Rainforest, Savannah, and Desert Ecosystems
  • Food Chains & Food Webs
  • Animal Behaviour & Enrichment

School Group Pricing

Prices are based on age group and include full-day zoo access.

School Age Group Price per Student Free Adults
Early Years / Nursery £6.00 1 free adult per 4 children
Primary (KS1–KS2) £7.50 1 free adult per 6 children
Secondary (KS3–KS4) £8.50 1 free adult per 10 students
Sixth Form / College £9.00 1 free adult per 12 students

Safety & Supervision

All school groups must be supervised at all times. Our staff are trained in animal welfare and visitor safety, and risk assessments are available upon request. We recommend planning your visit in advance to ensure a smooth and enjoyable day for everyone.

Booking Information

To book an educational visit, please contact our Education Team. We recommend booking at least four weeks in advance during busy seasons.
